About BharatJobAlert

BharatJobAlert is an independent informational website built to help Indian job seekers quickly discover central and state government recruitment notifications, exam dates, and official application portals.

What We Do

  • Organize public recruitment notices by organization (UPSC, SSC, RRB, Banking, Defence, and PSUs).
  • Filter openings by educational qualification (10th, 12th, Diploma, Graduation, and Post Graduation).
  • Link directly to official government recruitment websites so applicants never fall for fake forms.
